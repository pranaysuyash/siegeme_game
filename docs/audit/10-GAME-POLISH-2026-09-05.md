## Current game and intended experience

Sources: canonical handoff `../Siege-Me-Agent-Handoff/00_START_HERE.md`, full technical spec, procedural art direction, current audit `05`–`09`, live renderer/store/generator/ballistics and browser baseline.

One public fortress belongs to one ruler. Attackers obtain finite shots and lease a turn, aim ballistically, and progressively destroy structures. Defenders place finite shields/braces. The Core cannot heal; its decisive destruction determines succession. Reigns and contributions persist. A Cloudflare Durable Object owns commands and snapshots; D1 owns ledgers; Zustand projects confirmed state; Three.js renders it. Local visual changes must not introduce another gameplay world, award resources, or alter the collision/persistence schema.

The long-term value is a shared, watchable contest with legible strategy and memorable succession. The current product is an early playable browser implementation, not a completed AAA game. AAA is treated as a quality ambition across art direction, feel, completeness, accessibility, reliability, and performance, never as a release claim supported by screenshots.

## Accepted direction

Retain the compact procedural fortress. Develop an ancient coastal stronghold: weathered limestone, dark iron and bronze, amber standards and braziers, a turquoise Core, deep blue atmosphere, rock strata and a readable approach. Modular detail follows existing semantic components and disappears with their destruction. Preserve short trajectory previews and skill; do not expose the exact future target.

Improve the complete local loop: readable battlefield and objectives → discover attack/defense → adjustable aim and explicit fire alternative → truthful flight → distinct impact → persistent damage → next action. Keep progressive identity, finite resources, and succession intact. Add accessibility and quality controls where they serve this loop.

Alternatives: photorealistic castle/city rejected for this implementation because the current mobile-first compact arena and zero-required-model-pack direction favor authored procedural forms; external models remain an option for future hero assets. Exact targeting assistance rejected because the spec intentionally reserves collision judgment for the player. New economies/rewards are proposed research, not shortcuts to polish.

## Design research and the user's long-term question

The user asked which coaching choices have worked for games over the long term.
Recommendation: prioritize mastery, meaningful choice, and shared reign history.
This is a design judgment for Siege Me, not a measured retention outcome.

Primary sources inspected:

- [Player Experience of Needs Satisfaction, research overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/player-experience-of-needs-satisfaction-pens/): reports relationships between competence, autonomy, relatedness, enjoyment and continued play. The applicable principles are learnable controls, consistent feedback, useful choices and meaningful social participation. The proprietary questionnaire is not copied or implemented.
- [Kim Swift, Thinking with Portals: Creating Valve's New IP](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/thinking-with-portals-creating-valve-s-new-ip): the creator's account of early observational playtesting and introducing complex concepts in manageable steps. This supports testing comprehension repeatedly, not assuming a tutorial produces mastery.

Applied coaching priority: a live shield gets situational guidance; otherwise an
exposed Core gets an objective reminder; an almost-full shared charge introduces
the Power Orb; default guidance teaches walls → Core → throne. This is descriptive
and does not predict a hit. Aim sliders offer a precise alternative to dragging;
the short trajectory hint leaves the skill of choosing an arc intact.

Long-term product hypotheses, explicitly unimplemented:

- A free, clearly separate practice opportunity before a finite paid shot may
  improve informed choice and first-shot comprehension. It must never grant real
  world damage or disguise a purchase; test the learning benefit before changing
  the economy or public entry flow.
- Reign recaps, replays, contributor recognition and cosmetic identity may make
  participation meaningful beyond the final hit. Measure voluntary return and
  clarity, and avoid rewards that amplify spending advantages.
- Introduce tactical depth through readable tradeoffs before adding resources,
  loot, daily obligations, or more upgrade systems. More systems are not evidence
  of more durable enjoyment.

Suggested human playtest gates: watch without prompting; explain the objective;
aim and explain a miss; distinguish a shield from the Core/Orb; explain what
changed after a hit; find the next action; explain what survives succession.
Observe novices and returning players separately. Record confusion and recovery
before deciding numerical success thresholds from an actual baseline.
